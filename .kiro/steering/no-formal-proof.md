# Honest claims

- Never write "proven equivalent", "formal proof" or "guaranteed".
- VIVANT wording: "0 divergence found on N cases".
- ZOMBIE wording: "M divergence(s) found on N cases".
- Coverage wording: "Paragraph coverage X/Y" and "Branch coverage (Lazarus definition) X/Y". Lazarus definition: a branch is covered when its first statement appears in the trace of at least one case.
- Always show: "Validated under GnuCOBOL 3.x".
- AI explanations are labeled "AI explanation (advisory, does not affect verdict)". A cached explanation is labeled "Cached AI explanation, generated with Bedrock on [date]".
